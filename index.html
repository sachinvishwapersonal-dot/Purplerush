How This Resolves the Operational Bottleneck

❌ The Old Manual Problem

  • Customer books on bloorush.in.
  • Operator manually transcribes customer name, phone, address, and selected services into partnerflowbloo.com.
  • High risk of typos in contact info and incorrect service duration estimation.
  • 15–45 minute delays before partner is dispatched.
  • Manual labor costs scale linearly with order volume.

✅ The Refined Automated Solution

  • Direct Data Ingestion: Customer booking directly writes to the unified data store.
  • Smart Auto-Dispatch Engine: Matches present active partners by Hub zone and balances workload in < 1 second.
  • Automated WhatsApp Notifications: Dispatches job routes directly to field cleaners without manual intervention.
  • Live Tracking & Dynamic Incentive: Customer star ratings automatically calibrate partner bonuses.
